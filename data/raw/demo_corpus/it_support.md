# IT Support and Equipment

## Laptop replacement
For a damaged, failing or obsolete laptop, submit a hardware request in the IT support portal. Include the device asset tag, symptoms, urgency and whether the device can still be used. IT triages the request and may arrange troubleshooting, repair or replacement.

## Software requests
Request new software through the IT service portal. Provide the software name, business purpose and manager approval if required. IT and Security review licensing and risk before installation.

## Support tickets
Create a ticket for IT support and include steps to reproduce the problem, relevant screenshots and the impact on work. Avoid posting passwords, secret keys or sensitive personal information in tickets.
