# Leave and Time Off

## Annual leave
Employees accrue annual leave according to their employment agreement and applicable local law. Requests should be submitted in the HR portal at least two weeks before planned leave when practical. Managers review requests based on team coverage.

## Parental leave
Employees should contact People Operations as early as possible to discuss parental leave. The team provides guidance on eligibility, documentation and planning. Specific entitlement depends on jurisdiction and employment terms.

## Sick leave
Employees who are unable to work due to illness should notify their manager as soon as practical and follow local reporting requirements. Medical documentation may be required where local law or policy permits.
