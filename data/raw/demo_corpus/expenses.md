# Expenses and Reimbursements

## Expense submission
Employees submit business expenses through the expense portal. Include an itemized receipt, business purpose and project or cost centre where applicable. Submit expenses within 30 days of purchase unless local rules specify otherwise.

## Approval
The employee's manager reviews expenses. Expenses outside the standard policy or exceeding a limit require additional approval from Finance. Personal expenses are not reimbursable.

## Travel
Travel should be booked using the approved booking process when available. Employees should select reasonable options and retain receipts for reimbursable costs.
